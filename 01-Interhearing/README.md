# INTERHEARING

## Advertising & Landing Page Copywriting

**Project Type:** Advertising Copywriting | Landing Page Copywriting | Marketing Communication

**Industry:** Healthcare | Hearing Care Services

### 1. Project Overview

Interhearing is a hearing care provider offering professional hearing services and ear care solutions.

This project involved developing promotional messaging for two different marketing assets: a flyer advertisement promoting free hearing tests for adults aged 60 and above, and a landing page promoting microsuction ear wax removal services in Reading.

The objective was to communicate the services clearly, establish trust and encourage potential customers to take action.

### 2. Project Objectives

* Create attention-grabbing promotional messaging.
* Communicate service benefits in simple, accessible language.
* Build credibility through trust-focused messaging.
* Address potential customer concerns.
* Encourage enquiries and appointment bookings.

### 3. My Copywriting Approach

I approached the project by considering the target audience, the service being promoted and the action each marketing asset needed to encourage.

**Audience-focused messaging:** The content was tailored to communicate relevant benefits to people seeking hearing care and ear wax removal services.

**Clarity and simplicity:** I prioritised straightforward language to make the services and their benefits easy to understand.

**Trust and credibility:** The messaging incorporated professional qualifications, service information and reassurance to help build confidence.

**Conversion-focused writing:** I included clear calls to action to encourage visitors to call or book appointments.

### 4. Deliverable One: Promotional Flyer Advertisement

**Campaign:** Free Hearing Test for Adults Aged 60+

**Objective:** Generate awareness and encourage eligible adults to take advantage of the free hearing assessment.

**Copywriting Elements:**

* Attention-grabbing headline.
* Benefit-focused supporting message.
* Trust-building service highlights.
* Clear service information.
* Direct contact and booking details.

**Headline:**
FREE HEARING TEST FOR OVER 60s

**Supporting Message:**
Your hearing matters. Our free hearing test for adults aged 60 and over helps detect early signs of hearing loss, giving you clarity, confidence and peace of mind.

**Key Messaging Angles:**

* Professional hearing care.
* Modern testing equipment.
* Friendly, patient-focused service.
* Clear results and reassurance.

**CTA:** Visit Us or Book Online.

### 5. Deliverable Two: Landing Page Copywriting

**Campaign:** Microsuction Ear Wax Removal in Reading

**Objective:** Communicate the ear wax removal service, address common customer concerns and encourage appointment bookings.

**Copywriting Elements:**

* Service-focused hero headline.
* Supporting service description.
* Benefits and reasons to choose the clinic.
* Appointment process.
* Frequently asked questions.
* Location and contact information.
* Booking calls to action.

**Headline:**
Microsuction Ear Wax Removal in Reading

**Supporting Message:**
Safe, gentle ear wax removal carried out by qualified hearing professionals at our Reading clinic.

**Key Messaging Angles:**

* Professional treatment.
* Patient comfort.
* Convenient appointment options.
* Clear explanation of the service.
* Accessible clinic location.

**CTA:** Call Now | Book Online.
